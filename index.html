What this model knows about you: age, number of energy drinks, and game genre. What it doesn't know: your personal caffeine tolerance, actual resting heart rate, medication, anxiety, hydration, sleep, or prior habits. (Sleep was tested and dropped here: it has effectively zero relationship with heart rate in this dataset, correlation −0.0004, so including a sleep slider would have been misleading. Sleep does matter a lot for reaction time, see the Population Explorer tab.) The zone shown compares your estimated heart rate to real players your age in this dataset, it is not a medical reading of you specifically.
